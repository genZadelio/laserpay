QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked
questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Contact support.

What is Laserpay?

Laserpay is an integrated finance platform that helps businesses accept payments, make payouts, manage banking, automate payroll and access working capital from one place.

What services does Laserpay offer?

Laserpay offers payment gateways, links, pages, buttons, point-of-sale tools, subscriptions, international payments, business banking, payouts, payroll and flexible credit.

Which payment methods can my customers use?

Customers can pay using UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, wallets and supported international payment methods.

How long does integration take?

Most teams can create and test a payment in a few hours using our SDKs, plugins and quick-start guides.

Can I accept international payments?

Yes. Laserpay supports international cards and local methods across many markets, with settlement and currency controls built in.

Is my business data secure?

Laserpay is designed around encrypted data, role-based access, audit trails, risk controls and industry-standard security practices.

Does Laserpay support payroll compliance?

Yes. Payroll workflows include automated calculations, statutory deductions and support for compliance-ready reporting.
